Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Buna ("we", "our") collects when you use the Buna coffee platform at buna-ethiopia.web.app, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google to create your account.
  • Loyalty & rewards activity: points, digital stamps, streaks, raffle entries, and referral activity associated with your account or device.
  • Device identifier: a randomly generated ID stored in your browser's local storage, used to track guest loyalty progress before you sign in.
  • Community Wall posts: any text, photos, and display name you voluntarily submit are visible to other visitors.
  • Order and menu customization data: drink selections and order details you submit through the app.

How We Use This Information

  • To operate your loyalty account, track rewards, and personalize your experience.
  • To display community posts you choose to share publicly.
  • To operate core app functionality (menu, ordering, promotions).
  • To maintain and improve the security and reliability of the platform.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Data may be processed on Google's servers in accordance with Google Cloud's privacy practices.

Your Choices

  • You can use most of Buna as a guest without signing in.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
  • Community Wall posts are public once submitted; contact us to request removal.

Children's Privacy

Buna is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
